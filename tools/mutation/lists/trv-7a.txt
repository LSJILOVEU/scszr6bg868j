# ring, order, own nodes), the wiring's placeholders and build time, and the two sight rules the table relies on (combat's
# static sight: units never block it, plates do). Filter: the TRV-7a tests.
# Not listed: the appendix row "three costs added for a walk through an entry" (TRV-7b). Ring bounds are checked on whole
# metres in the map tests and on raw values in T7-10; a cell 1 raw off a bound does not exist on a 1 m grid.
