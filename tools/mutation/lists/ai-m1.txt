# AI-M1: melee presses (PressureRules.Strikes, content field pressureStrikes) and a pressed melee unit asks the engage part
# before it chases (content field meleeNerve); told to take cover, it still goes for an enemy within meleeNerveFightMm. Filters: the AI-M1 unit tests (pressure, scheduler, content and state).
# Not listed: the damage test of a landed strike (a strike without damage adds nothing to the sum, so the unit falls or
# not as without the test); the shots left out of the sum for a unit the strikes hit (a unit the fire hit is counted by
# then, or its shots and strikes together fall short, so the strikes alone give the same answer); Kills itself
# (pf-kills-* in base-1.txt); a strike whose striker or target is gone read as
# present (the lookup throws: an exception, not an assertion); the saved flag not read back (the reader runs out of step:
# an exception); the melee-nerve tag folded after the layers tag (the pressure fields are refused on floor plates, so no
# world has both).
