# ML-5: the plate-crossing test (Sim/PlateField) and its bridge from a .wcmap. Filter: the ML-5 tests.
# Not listed as equivalent: Leaving() for an axis that stays out of the map (Cell already refuses cells off the map),
# the start-outside jump on the + side (walking cell by cell from outside gives the same answer), and a corner with
# only + or only - crossings visited at a single point (that point is the next or the current cell anyway). Loosening the
# off-map guard in Cell() indexes past the cell table and throws instead of answering wrong, so it is not listed either.
# Also equivalent: SameT compared in long (equal fractions stay equal mod 2^64, and unequal ones would need a difference
# that is a multiple of 2^64); the early stop before a corner cell and before the end cell at t = 1 on a tie (the cell
# before it has positive length, so its best t* is strictly below the crossing; a tie at the crossing cannot happen there).
# ml5-49 to ml5-58 were added in review round 1.
