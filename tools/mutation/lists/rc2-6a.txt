# RC2-6a: squad members squeeze past their standing leader. Two places: the walking code marks the controlled unit as
# walking on a tick in which its input gives it a step (InterimLocomotion.Crowd.cs), and the rule with a led squad lets a
# member pass the standing leader as two team mates pass each other (CrowdPassFeature.cs, TeamIdleCrowdPass).
# Filters: Rc26aWalkingLeaderTests for the walking code; for the rule the two tests of Rc21LedSquadTests that pin it
# (TheLeaderPasses..., TheSquadFollowsItsLeaderUpTheStair) and Wp21PassTests.
# The rule's digest value is the entry rc2-1-pass-digest-value of rc2-1.txt, quoted anew.
# Not listed: the two checks that the rule has a controlled unit at all (a rule made without one only throws there, no
# assertion fails first); asking the step after the drags instead of the input's own (no test world has a drag that makes
# a step zero); a squad whose leader is not the controlled unit (the led squad steps before the two systems that change the
# body and disbands as soon as its leader is dead, so the walking step never sees one).
