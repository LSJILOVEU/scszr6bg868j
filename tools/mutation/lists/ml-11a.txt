# ML-11a: the AI by layer. One slot index over ground and plate slots, arriving and orders with a layer, points compared by
# walking cost (LayeredReach per point and per base), points out of reach passed over, the refusals on a map with plates,
# the digest tag. Filter: the ML-11a tests and the re-recorded ML-9b match test.
# Not listed: GuardSlots.AllAt and AllCanonical with "<=" at the table boundary (the mutant reads past the ground table: an
# exception, not an assertion; AllLayerOf carries the boundary); the squared cost replaced by the cost (same order); "<"
# for "<=" in the relaxation (the same value written once more); a spread from the front-line point falling back to the unit
# (a front-line point reaches a point by definition); the scheduler's arrays sized by Total (an exception); the layer
# dropped from "is the unit already walking to this slot" (the same place on another layer has another height, so the
# targets differ anyway).
